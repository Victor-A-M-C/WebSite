The Course of Empire

2-9-2026

An uncertain dawn among forest growth,
Wind blowing on embers starting a flame,
An unspoken bounding immortal oath,
Allowing the birth of a sturdy frame.

Vibrant fabrics streaming through open air,
Gold clad parades strutting through busy streets
With nary a worry nary a care
Boasting about all of their striking feats.

A ceaseless wave of unbounded despair
The consequence of actions forgotten.
Reduced to a state of mere disrepair
A once happy memory is rotten.

As nature returns to abandoned coast,
It hugs the shell of its vacant host.

The Sailor

21-8-2026

There was once a simple sailor
Who was lost alone at sea,
And to ensure that he would not waiver,
He thought of his bride to be.

And through vicious days and horrid nights
When the waves forced him to his knee,
The only thing that could stop his fright
Was the thought of his sweet chérie.

At last he finally arrived
And waited by their tree,
But to his surprise he learned she’d died
And jumped into the sea.
